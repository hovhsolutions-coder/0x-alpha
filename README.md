## What's actually known about it

No hype beyond the facts — because half the fun of a stealth model is that nobody's confirmed the rest:

- **1.05M-token context**, up to **131K tokens of output** — enormous even by frontier standards
- **Multimodal** — takes text, images, and video
- **Built for long-horizon software engineering** — sustained, multi-step agentic coding and terminal workflows, not one-shot answers
- **Strong early reviews** — the OpenCode community jumped on it fast; early testers report it handling multi-step refactoring and bug-fixing favorably against frontier models
- **Preview privacy terms** — requests are retained for the trial period but, per the stated terms, not used to train the model
- **Free while in preview** — available across OpenRouter and OpenCode Zen at no cost during the trial
Who made it? Officially unknown. The community has theories; we're not going to pretend to settle it. What we can do is give you the best way to actually *use* it while it's here.

- **Multimodal in** — drop screenshots, diagrams, or video frames straight into the workflow
